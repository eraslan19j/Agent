# My Vision — Google Play English listing

**App name:** My Vision: Photo Studio

**Short description:** Looks, precise edits, text and collages in a free, on-device studio.

## Full description

Create your vision with a focused photo studio that runs on your device.

Import or take a photo, edit it and save the result. No account, payment or cloud service is required.

LOOKS AND PRECISE COLOR
• Adjustable signature looks
• Exposure, brightness, contrast, saturation and temperature
• Three-zone curves and seven-channel HSL
• Radial and linear selective masks

FRAME AND DETAIL
• Crop, rotate, flip, straighten and perspective correction
• Noise reduction, sharpening, vignette and film grain
• Live histogram, undo/redo and before/after comparison

EDITABLE TEXT
Select a text layer again to change its content, position, size, color, font, alignment and rotation. Long text wraps within the image instead of being cut off.

COLLAGES AND BATCH EXPORT
Create real collages with 2–6 photos. Apply shared editing settings across multiple photos and export them together.

LOCAL PROJECTS
Search, filter, favorite and organize real projects with albums and tags. Media blobs and project metadata are stored locally in IndexedDB. Export important work as a separate backup.

HIGH-QUALITY OUTPUT
Choose JPG or PNG, quality and output size up to 4K. Share through the Android share sheet or Web Share API.

FREE AND ON DEVICE
Photos are not uploaded to My Vision servers for editing. This version has no advertising trackers, account, payment or cloud requirement. Use light, dark or system theme and a Turkish or English interface.
