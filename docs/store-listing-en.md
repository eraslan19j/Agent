# My Vision — Google Play English listing

**App name (30 characters):** My Vision: Photo Studio

**Short description (80 characters):** Pro looks, curves, retouching, collages and private on-device photo editing.

## Full description

Create your vision with a complete photo studio that runs on your device.

My Vision combines precise editing with a focused, modern workflow. Import your own photos, take a picture or choose a credited image from Discover. No sample projects are placed in your archive.

EDIT WITH CONTROL
• Signature looks with adjustable intensity
• Exposure, color, texture, vignette and film grain
• Three-zone curves and seven-channel HSL color
• Radial and linear selective masks
• Noise reduction and sharpening
• Crop, rotate, straighten and perspective correction
• Live histogram, undo/redo and before/after comparison

RETOUCH AND CREATE
• On-device blemish, red-eye, color and small-object touch-up
• Text, stickers, vector brushes and frames
• Custom editing recipes
• Real multi-photo collages
• Batch exports with a shared recipe and optional watermark
• On-device background-color removal with transparent PNG export

YOUR PHOTOS STAY YOURS
Editing runs locally. Original photos and projects are not uploaded to My Vision servers. Discover accesses Lorem Picsum only when you use that feature. No advertising trackers.

ORGANIZE YOUR WORK
Search your real projects, mark favorites and add albums or tags. Settings include dark, AMOLED, light and system themes, multiple accents, reduced blur and Turkish/English interfaces.

FREE AND PRO
Basic editing and standard-size exports are free. My Vision Pro unlocks premium looks and 4K export. Price, billing period and any trial are supplied by Google Play before purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Google Play account.

Classical background removal and touch-up tools are on-device image-processing features; they are not presented as generative AI.
