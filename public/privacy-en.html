My Vision — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains how My Vision handles your photos and app data.

Summary: Private projects are processed on your device and stored in IndexedDB. Only photos you explicitly upload to the sharing space are sent to the server and shown in the public gallery.

1. Photos and projects

Selected photos, edit settings and thumbnails are kept in your device’s IndexedDB storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may delete projects. Export important work to keep a backup.

2. Offline operation

Looks, color adjustments, text, collages, batch exports and video encoding are generated on your device. There is no account or social feed. The optional sharing space adds only photos you upload to the server’s public gallery.

3. Camera, gallery and sharing

Camera or gallery access is used only when you choose a photo. Operating-system sharing and “Upload to sharing space” occur only when you initiate them. Photos and notes in the sharing space are visible to everyone who can view the gallery; do not upload sensitive or private content.

4. Analytics and advertising

This version contains no behavioral advertising, ad trackers or third-party analytics SDK. Project counts are calculated on your device.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete local projects in the app. Deletion removes the IndexedDB record and associated photo copy. Copies you previously exported remain under your control. Content uploaded to the sharing space may remain stored until removed by the server administrator; use the contact channel below for deletion requests.

6. Children’s privacy

The app works without an account. Do not upload photos or personal information of children under 13 to the public sharing space.

7. Changes and contact

The date of any policy update will be published on this page. Questions may be submitted through the project’s GitHub issues page.
