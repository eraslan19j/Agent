My Vision — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains how My Vision handles your photos and app data.

Summary: Photos are processed on your device. Projects are stored as Blobs in IndexedDB and are not uploaded to a My Vision server.

1. Photos and projects

Selected photos, edit settings and thumbnails are kept in your device’s IndexedDB storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may delete projects. Export important work to keep a backup.

2. Offline operation

Looks, color adjustments, text, collages and batch exports are generated on your device. Phase 1 contains no account, cloud gallery, social feed or online media service.

3. Camera, gallery and sharing

Camera or gallery access is used only when you choose a photo. File sharing occurs only when you initiate it through the operating system’s share interface.

4. Analytics and advertising

This version contains no behavioral advertising, ad trackers or third-party analytics SDK. Project counts are calculated on your device.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete projects in the app. Deletion removes the IndexedDB record and associated photo copy. Copies you previously exported remain under your control.

6. Children’s privacy

The app works without an account and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes and contact

The date of any policy update will be published on this page. Questions may be submitted through the project’s GitHub issues page.
