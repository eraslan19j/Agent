Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains how the My Vision mobile app handles your photos and app data.

Summary: Photos you choose to edit are processed on your device. We do not upload your photos to our servers, sell them or use them to build advertising profiles.

1. Photos and projects

Selected photos, edits and saved projects remain in the app’s local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may delete these records. You control exported copies.

2. Discover and network access

Discover connects to Lorem Picsum (picsum.photos) only when you use it. That provider may process your IP address and technical request data. Gallery and project photos are not sent to it. A public Discover image is downloaded only when you choose to edit it.

3. Camera and gallery

Camera or gallery access is used only when you start taking or selecting a photo. The app does not scan your entire gallery or upload photos in the background.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

For paid Google Play subscriptions, RevenueCat validates purchase status. An app user ID, subscription status and device/platform information may be processed. My Vision does not store payment card details; Google Play manages payments.

5. Analytics and advertising

This version contains no behavioral advertising, ad trackers or third-party analytics SDK. Project counts are calculated on your device.

6. Data sharing

Your photos are not shared with third parties. Limited purchase data required for subscription validation is processed by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own privacy policies.

7. Security and retention

Local projects use operating-system storage protections. Device backup is disabled. Purchase records may be retained by the relevant providers as required for legal and subscription-validation purposes.

8. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Parents who believe a child has supplied data may contact us.

9. Your rights and contact

You can delete local projects in the app. For privacy or subscription-data questions, contact support@myvision.app.

10. Changes

The update date will be published on this page when this policy changes. Material changes may also be announced in the app.
